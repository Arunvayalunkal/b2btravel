FAQ
1. How do I create a B2Btravel.com account?

Creating an account with B2Btravel.com is easy. Simply click the 'Register' button on our home page, fill in your details, and create your User ID and password to complete the registration process. It only takes a few minutes

2. Can I change my password?

Yes, you certainly can. Changing your password is a simple process that you can do on your own by clicking on “Forgot Password”, or you can call us for assistance if needed.

3. What should I do if I forget my password?

Forgot your password? No need to worry. Simply fill in your agency ID and click the 'Forgot Password' link under the login button. Your password will be sent to your registered email
